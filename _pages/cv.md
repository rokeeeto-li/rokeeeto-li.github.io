Research Interests
======
My research focuses on robot collaboration: enabling robots to work with teammates, both other robots and humans, through perception, task allocation, and coordinated task execution. I draw on large-scale pre-trained models, learning-based perception, and planning to operate under uncertainty and limited communication, and I am passionate about making my algorithms run reliably and efficiently on real robots.

Research Experience
======
**Research Associate, CoRob Lab**, North Carolina State University, Jul 2025 -- Present<br>
Advisor: Dr. Peng Gao
* **Collaborative Perception:** Developing vision-based perception for robot teams, including communication-efficient relative pose estimation between robots under limited bandwidth and intermittent shared views. [[website](https://rokeeeto-li.github.io/cerpe.github.io/)] [[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14539)]
* **Robot Collaboration:** Developing task coordination for a hybrid robot team of tabletop manipulators, mobile robots, and mobile manipulators, building on preliminary work in long-horizon task and motion planning.
* **Real-Robot Deployment:** Independently ran real-world experiments across multiple projects on a Unitree quadruped, manipulators, a mobile manipulator, and wheeled robots, and built the perception and navigation stacks.

**Research Associate, Mazurowski Lab**, Duke University, Oct 2024 -- Jun 2025<br>
Advisor: Dr. Maciej A. Mazurowski
* Contributed to MRI-CORE, a large-scale pre-trained model for MRI that transfers to data-restricted segmentation, classification, and zero-shot segmentation tasks. [[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12186)]
* Contributed to multiple other projects, including volumetric image annotation with SAM 2, breast MRI registration (Best Paper Award, MICCAI DeepBreath Workshop 2025), and CT-based bone loss measurement.
* Developed BreastSegNet, a multi-label segmentation model for breast MRI. [[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.13604)]

**Research Associate, [SAIR Lab](https://sairlab.org/)**, University at Buffalo, Aug 2023 -- Sep 2024<br>
Advisors: Dr. Chen Wang and Dr. Junyi Geng
* Enhanced the Model Predictive Control (MPC) module in the open-source library PyPose, enabling robust operation and highly efficient differentiation. [[website](https://pypose.org/)] [[paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.13035.pdf)]
* Developed an end-to-end training pipeline that embeds MPC within the neural network, enabling explicit prediction of kinematically feasible trajectories for motion planning. [[website](https://sairlab.org/iKap/)] [[paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09496)]

**Research Assistant, [BIGSS Lab](https://bigss.lcsr.jhu.edu/)**, Johns Hopkins University, Sep 2022 -- Jul 2023<br>
Advisor: Dr. Mehran Armand
* Developed RGB camera-based state-estimation tools for Continuum Dexterous Manipulators (CDMs).
* Designed a novel capacitance-based sensor for CDM state estimation at 1% of the cost of the original solution, with sub-centimeter accuracy on a prototype ten times larger. [[paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10324929)]
