Hi, I am Qihang Li, a PhD student in Computer Science at North Carolina State University, advised by Dr. Peng Gao in the CoRob Lab. Before NC State, I earned my MSE in Robotics from Johns Hopkins University and my BEng in Mechatronics and Robotics System from a joint program between Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU) and the University of Liverpool.

Research Interests
-----
My research focuses on **robot collaboration**: enabling robots to work with teammates, both other robots and humans, through perception, task allocation, and coordinated task execution. I draw on large-scale pre-trained models, learning-based perception, and planning to operate under uncertainty and limited communication. I am also passionate about making my algorithms run reliably and efficiently on **real robots**, and I have deployed my work on quadrupeds, manipulators, mobile manipulators, and wheeled platforms.

Recent work includes communication-efficient relative pose estimation for collaborative perception ([project page](https://rokeeeto-li.github.io/cerpe.github.io/)), and task coordination for hybrid robot teams.

Background
-----
Before joining the CoRob Lab, I worked on robot planning and learning at the [Spatial AI and Robotics (SAIR) Lab](https://sairlab.org/) at the University at Buffalo, contributing to [PyPose](https://pypose.org/) and [iKap](https://sairlab.org/iKap/). At Duke University, I worked on medical image analysis in the Mazurowski Lab, including large-scale pre-trained models for MRI. At the [Laboratory of Biomechanical and Image Guided Surgical Systems (BIGSS)](https://bigss.lcsr.jhu.edu/) at Johns Hopkins, I worked on sensing and perception for continuum manipulators in medical robotics.
