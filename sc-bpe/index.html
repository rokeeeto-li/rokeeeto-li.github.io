Overview

Mobile manipulators usually pick a base pose for the next skill and reposition for the one after. Often a single pose could support the whole task, but finding it is hard: grasps and arm motions are not yet decided, and early choices change what later skills can reach.

SC-BPE is a learned evaluator that scores a base pose together with a plan skeleton, the ordered Pick and Place actions with their objects and regions. A task and motion planner then refines the highest-scoring pairs first, keeping the base fixed for the whole sequence.

In one line: predict whether the whole plan can work from one base pose before spending time on motion planning.

Method

The evaluator scores a base pose together with the whole plan skeleton, so the same symbolic plan can rank differently from different bases.

Overview

Given a task goal and an RGB-D observation, a symbolic planner proposes plan skeletons. SC-BPE scores each skeleton together with a sampled base pose, and the TAMP planner refines the most promising pairs first, with the base fixed for the whole sequence.

Results

With one fixed base pose, SC-BPE plans faster than random ordering of the same candidates, its scores follow how the plan changes the best base, and it is competitive with methods that move the base between skills.

1. Does ranking make planning faster?

Yes. SC-BPE has higher success and lower planning time than random ordering of the same candidates in all eight settings. On Task 2–O3 it solves all 29 scenes (random: 28.3%), and its first attempt already succeeds in 86.2% of scenes (random: 2.1%).

Task 2–O3: cumulative success versus (a) candidate attempts and (b) planning time, on 29 test scenes (60 s per candidate, 180 s per scene).

2. Do the scores reflect the plan?

Yes. The three skeletons below differ only in the temporary buffer, yet get different score maps. Successful base positions sit toward the left for B1, the right for B3, and a broader central region for B2, and the high-score regions follow.

3. How does a fixed base compare with rebasing?

It is competitive. SC-BPE has higher success and lower planning time than both rebasing methods on Task 1. On Task 2–O4 it ties Per-Skill Rebase at 89.7% success while cutting mean planning time from 124.4 to 75.0 s. Rebasing can win when moving helps: on Task 3–O2, Per-Skill Rebase reaches 96.6% success against 86.2%.
